# Microtech Inc. — Corporate Tax & Book-to-Tax Analysis

> **Finance & Tax Analysis Portfolio Synthetic Project**
> Corporate Tax • Book-to-Tax Reconciliation • Tax Adjustments • Excel • Financial Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an end-to-end **corporate tax and book-to-tax adjustment analysis** for Microtech Inc., demonstrating how financial accounting information can be reconciled to federal taxable income.

The analysis focuses on identifying and classifying **permanent and temporary book-to-tax differences**, evaluating depreciation timing differences, calculating federal taxable income and tax liability, and translating the analysis into practical tax-compliance process improvements.

The project was developed as a portfolio case study to demonstrate practical application of **accounting, corporate tax, financial analysis, Excel, and professional reporting skills**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Map financial accounts to appropriate Balance Sheet and Income Statement categories
* Identify and classify permanent and temporary book-to-tax differences
* Distinguish favorable and unfavorable tax adjustments
* Reconcile pre-tax book income to federal taxable income
* Calculate the resulting federal tax liability
* Analyze depreciation-related timing differences
* Translate tax analysis into practical process-improvement opportunities

## 🔎 Book-to-Tax Difference Analysis

The project separates book-to-tax differences into **permanent** and **temporary** categories.

### Permanent Differences

**Net adjustment: +$312,000**

Examples analyzed include:

* Federal income tax expense
* Meals & entertainment
* Club dues / social organization expenses
* Tax-exempt / municipal bond interest

Permanent differences do not reverse in future periods.

### Temporary Differences

**Net adjustment: −$251,900**

Examples analyzed include:

* Depreciation
* Accrued bonus
* Prepaid rent and insurance

Temporary differences arise from timing differences between book and tax treatment and can reverse in future periods.

## 🏗️ Depreciation Timing Analysis

A **$50,000 asset** was used to demonstrate the difference between book and tax depreciation.

| 2025                   |        Amount |
| ---------------------- | ------------: |
| Book Depreciation      |       $10,000 |
| Tax Depreciation       |       $50,000 |
| Book-to-Tax Difference | **($40,000)** |

The analysis demonstrates how accelerated tax depreciation creates a favorable timing difference in 2025, followed by reversals in subsequent periods.
